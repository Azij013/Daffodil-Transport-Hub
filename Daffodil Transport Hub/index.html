Notice Board

December 15, 2024
Important

Holiday Schedule Update

Transport services will operate on modified schedule during winter break (December 20 - January 5). Limited routes will be available with reduced frequency. Please check the updated schedule before planning your journey.

December 12, 2024
New

New Route Added: Mirpur to DSC

We're excited to announce a new route connecting Mirpur to DSC campus. The service will start from December 18, 2024, with departures at 7:45 AM and 1:45 PM from DSC, and return trips at 9:45 AM and 4:45 PM.

December 10, 2024
Update

Mobile App Launch

The DIU Transport mobile app is now available for download on both Android and iOS platforms. Book tickets, track buses in real-time, and manage your travel preferences on the go.

December 8, 2024
Reminder

Student ID Required for All Journeys

Please remember to carry your valid student ID card for all bus journeys. Students without proper identification may be denied boarding. Faculty and staff should carry their respective ID cards.

December 5, 2024
Event

Special Transport for Convocation

Additional buses will be arranged for the upcoming convocation ceremony on December 22, 2024. Special routes will operate from major residential areas to the main campus. Registration required.
